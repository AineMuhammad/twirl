# Sample model credits

The demo models below come from the
[Khronos glTF Sample Assets](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets) repository.
**Changes made to every model:** split into named parts, hierarchy flattened, rescaled to real-world
size, cameras, lights, animations and material variants removed, and compressed (Meshopt geometry,
WebP textures). Further changes are noted per model. Product names, descriptions and prices in the
demo are fictional.
